# Contributing

Keep contributions short, defensive and understandable.

## A useful contribution

- solves a real exposure problem;
- can be used without specialist tools;
- separates observed facts from assumptions;
- ends with a concrete action;
- protects the person instead of blaming them.

## Out of scope

- intrusive collection;
- surveillance or evasion instructions;
- targeting real people;
- exploit code, stolen data or private credentials;
- another scoring system or theoretical framework.

## Propose a change

Open an issue or pull request and answer three questions:

1. What problem does this solve?
2. Who will use it?
3. What can they do differently after reading it?
