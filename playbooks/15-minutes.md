# Review an exposure in 15 minutes

Use this when a person receives privileged access, takes a visible role or is about to attend a public event.

## 0–2 minutes — What matters?

Write down:

- the person or role;
- the resource, decision or activity at stake;
- the most serious realistic consequence.

Do not start searching until this is clear.

## 2–8 minutes — What is visible?

Look as an unknown outsider would, using public sources only:

- name and common username;
- employer, role and public biography;
- public social profiles and recent posts;
- conference pages, interviews and documents;
- public code contributions or technical references;
- visible places, relationships and timing.

Record facts and links. Do not collect unrelated personal information.

## 8–11 minutes — What can be connected?

Build no more than three short chains:

`source → fact → possible use → consequence`

Mark each conclusion as **fact**, **likely** or **unknown**.

## 11–13 minutes — What matters most?

Keep only findings that reveal:

- access;
- authority;
- a trusted relationship;
- a place;
- a useful moment.

## 13–15 minutes — What changes now?

Choose up to three actions:

- one thing to remove or reduce;
- one connection to separate or delay;
- one verification or response step to prepare.

Document the result in the [exposure review template](../templates/exposure-review.md).

The review is complete when someone can explain the risk and the next action in plain language.
